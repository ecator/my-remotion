# What do you want to make?

> Pick the kind of video you want, from what you already have: a website, an idea, footage, a pull request, music or data.

Pick what you're starting from. Each guide shows finished videos, the requests that made them, and how to change them.

<video aria-label="Reference any video: copy its editing style, choose who's on camera, say what it's about" src="https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/docs/studio-demos/reference-demo-v2.mp4" poster="/public/images/studio-app/reference-demo-v2.jpg" muted loop playsInline preload="metadata" />

**Have a video you like?** Start from it instead of choosing. In the Studio app, open **Reference** on Home and paste
its link (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or X), upload it, or pick one from the feed. The app copies its cuts, captions and
effects (or only its motion graphics), asks who's on camera (you, or an AI avatar), and lets you swap the parts that
should be yours. A video you only half like is still a better start than a blank page.

**Not sure which?** Describe the whole video in your own words. The agent works out the kind of video from what you ask
for. With your own agent, start the message with `/hyperframes`.

Coming from Remotion? [Port a Remotion composition](https://hyperframes.heygen.com/guides/hyperframes-vs-remotion.md).
